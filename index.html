LivePublic safety

BreakBoard

Better breaks. Smoother shifts. One board the whole room can see, so nobody has to ask who is out or who goes next.

Open BreakBoard
  • Leads and dispatchers. Two roles, each seeing the controls they need.
  • Planned breaks. Pick a time and a length and the slot is held for you.
  • A warning before time is up. Five minutes' notice, then a short grace period.
  • Extra breaks on request. Ask for one and a lead approves or declines it.
  • Your own board address. A short link for the team, with sign-in by name and password.
In developmentPublic safety

CrossCall

When a caller is in another jurisdiction, you need that center's direct line now. CrossCall, by Dispatch Atlas, is the directory for that moment.

  • Search by city, county or agency. Narrow by state when a name is common.
  • Every number shows its source. With the date it was last verified.
  • Built on the FCC's PSAP registry. Secondary and orphaned centers are labeled as such.
  • Verified users only. Sign in by emailed link. Approved agency domains get in at once, everyone else is reviewed.
  • Report a wrong number. Corrections go to an admin with a source attached.
AlphaWriting

Draftbound

The familiarity of a word processor, except the document knows it's a novel. Simple when you just want to write, with deeper tools when you want them.

  • A real manuscript editor. Fixed pages, chapters and parts, focus mode, comments and footnotes.
  • Story tools beside the text. Characters, worldbuilding, notes, and plot as a board, outline or timeline.
  • Continuity tracking. See who appears in which chapter and what was established where.
  • Import and export. Bring in a Word file, and export Word, PDF or standard manuscript format.
  • Hard to lose work. Autosave, recovery history and a 30-day trash.

Honse SOP

A procedure only helps if people have read the current version. Honse SOP makes that visible.

  • Drafts and versions. Edit privately, then publish with a note on what changed.
  • Read receipts. Each new version asks everyone to read and acknowledge it again.
  • Full history. Every version is kept, with changes shown against the one before.
  • Writing checks. A live score flags vague wording, missing sections and unmarked hazards.
  • Review reminders. Set a review cycle and overdue procedures are flagged.

About Honse

Honse LLC is a small, independent software company. We would rather make a few tools that each do one job well than one platform that does everything.

Two of them are for 911 dispatch, because that is the work we know best. The other two are for people who write, whether that is a novel or a procedure.
